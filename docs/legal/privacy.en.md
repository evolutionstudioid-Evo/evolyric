## 1. Introduction

Welcome to EvoLyric.

EvoLyric is a digital notepad app that lets users create, save, organise, display and share notes, including song lyrics, across devices.

EvoLyric is developed and operated by Evolution Digital Studio, based in Indonesia.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information and the content you store in the app. This policy explains what we process, why, where it is stored, and what rights you have over your data.

## 2. Information We Process

### A. Account information

If you sign in with Google, we receive from Google's sign-in service:

- Your Google email address.
- Your display name.
- Your profile photo.
- The account identifier needed for sign-in.

EvoLyric never receives or stores your Google password.

### B. Profile information

Signed-in users can have a profile with:

- A username.
- The display name and profile photo of the Google account.
- A bio you write yourself, up to 300 characters.

Your username, display name, profile photo and bio can be seen by your friends, and by other users who search for your exact username. There is no list of users to browse.

Your email address is never shown to other users.

### C. Library and notes

What you store can include:

- Note titles.
- Artist names or supporting information.
- Note content (for example lyrics).
- Categories.
- Section labels, annotations, colours and note settings (for example speed and text size).

Your notes belong to you. Evolution Digital Studio claims no ownership of the content you enter.

### D. Friends and sharing

When you use Friends, the system processes:

- Usernames and profile information.
- Friend requests and friendship status.
- Notes or categories sent and received (title, artist, content, key and colours). Annotations are not sent.

### E. Safety and moderation

To prevent abuse, the system processes:

- The users you have blocked.
- Reports you send: the usernames of the reporter and the reported user, the reason, any details you write, and an excerpt of a reported delivery as evidence.
- Activity timestamps (friend requests, deliveries and reports) to enforce daily limits.
- Account suspension status, with its duration and reason.

### F. Support requests

If you contact us with the Contact support button, the email automatically includes the app version, your device and browser type, and your username (if any). You can see and change the email before sending it.

## 3. Guest Use and Google Sign-In

You can use EvoLyric **without signing in**, as a guest. As a guest you can create, save, organise and display notes, search lyrics through LRCLIB and make backups. All guest data is stored **only on that device**.

Google sign-in is required to:

- Save your Library to your account and sync it to the cloud through Supabase.
- Use the same Library on other devices.
- Use Friends (adding friends, sending and receiving notes).

The first time a guest signs in on a device, the guest notes on that device are merged into the account once.

## 4. Data Storage

### A. On your device

The following is stored on your device so the app works offline: your Library, categories, recently opened songs, queue, display and language preferences, and your sign-in session (if signed in).

### B. In the cloud

For signed-in users, the Library, profile and required data are stored and synced through Supabase. The EvoLyric Supabase project is located in **Tokyo, Japan**. At database level, each account can only access its own data.

Syncing requires an internet connection. Local data that has not synced yet can be lost if browser or app data is cleared, the device is damaged, or local storage is wiped. Guest data is never synced.

## 5. Backup, Restore and Export

EvoLyric provides:

- **Backup**: one **.evolyric** file with all your notes and categories.
- **Restore**: brings back a .evolyric file without overwriting notes you already have.
- **Export .txt**: all notes in one text file.

Exported files are entirely under your control. You are responsible for keeping them safe.

## 6. Note Privacy and Friends

Your notes are private. Other users cannot see them unless you send them. EvoLyric has no public space or public feed.

As the service operator, Evolution Digital Studio technically has access to the database. We do not open user content except when needed for support you asked for, service security, or to follow up a report of abuse.

Friends works like this:

1. A user sends a friend request.
2. The recipient must accept it.
3. Once connected, users can send notes or categories to specific friends.

Sending makes a copy; it does not move anything. The original stays in the sender's Library, and the recipient decides whether to save the copy. Deliveries not yet saved are deleted if the friendship ends, either side blocks the other, or either account is deleted. Copies the recipient has already saved remain in the recipient's Library.

You can **block** other users. A blocked person can no longer find you, send you requests or send you notes, and is not told. You can also **report** users or deliveries that break the rules.

## 11. Retention and Deletion

Account and Library data are kept while the account is active.

You can **delete your account yourself** in **Account Settings → Delete account**. Before deletion the app shows a warning and asks you to type a confirmation word. You can also request deletion at evolution.studio.id@gmail.com.

When an account is deleted, the following is removed permanently and immediately:

- The sign-in account.
- All notes and categories in the cloud.
- Profile, username and bio.
- Friends, friend requests, deliveries not yet received, the block list, activity records and suspension status.
- The copy of the Library on the device where the deletion was made.

Reports made by or about the account are kept for moderation, but the deleted account's username and content excerpts are removed from them.

There is no recovery period: deleted accounts and data cannot be restored by you or by Evolution Digital Studio. System backups held by infrastructure providers (if any) are removed on the provider's retention cycle.

Not deleted:

- Copies of notes previously sent to and saved by other users.
- Backup or export files you saved yourself.
- Data on other devices that have not opened the app since the deletion; it stays until you sign out or clear the app's data on that device.

Guest data exists only on the device and can be removed by clearing the app's or browser's data.

## 12. Security

We apply security measures appropriate to the service and its infrastructure, including:

- Sign-in through Google; EvoLyric stores no passwords.
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS).
- Database-level access rules so each account can only access its own data.
- Email addresses are never shown to other users.
- Daily limits, blocking, reports and account suspension against abuse.

Still, no storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely risk-free. You are responsible for keeping your Google account and devices secure.

## 13. Children's Privacy
