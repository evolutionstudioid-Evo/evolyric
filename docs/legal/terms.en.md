## 2. The EvoLyric Service

EvoLyric's main functions include:

- Creating and editing notes, including section labels that can be added automatically (Auto Section).
- Saving notes to a Library and grouping them in categories.
- Displaying notes with auto-scroll, setlists and a queue.
- Offline access through local storage.
- Cloud sync (requires Google sign-in).
- Sending and receiving notes with Friends (requires Google sign-in).
- Searching lyrics through external services (LRCLIB and Google Search).

- Blocking and reporting other users.

1. Other users can only be found by typing their exact username.
2. Friend requests must be accepted by the recipient.
3. You can only send to friends you are connected with.
4. A sent note is a copy; the original is not moved.
5. The recipient decides whether to save the copy to their Library.
